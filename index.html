Temples of Nepal

Architectural Wonders of the Kathmandu Valley

Pashupatinath Temple

Situated on the banks of the sacred Bagmati River, Pashupatinath is one of the most significant Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva worldwide. Featuring a double-roofed pagoda built with gilded copper and intricate wood carvings.

Nyatapola Temple

Standing tall in Bhaktapur Taumadhi Square, Nyatapola is a 5-tier pagoda built in 1702 AD. It remains the tallest temple structure in Nepal, famous for its formidable structural integrity and guardian statues lining its steps.

Boudhanath Stupa

Dominating the Kathmandu skyline with its giant mandala construction, Boudhanath is a beacon of Tibetan Buddhism. The iconic eyes of the Buddha gaze outward in four directions symbolizing wisdom and compassion.
